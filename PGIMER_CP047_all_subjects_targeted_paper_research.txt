PGIMER COMPUTER PROGRAMMER CP/047
TARGETED QUESTION-PAPER / SUBJECT RESEARCH PACK
Research date: 6 September 2026

MISSION
The goal is NOT to collect every computer-related government paper.
The goal is to collect papers that can help predict and train for the PGIMER
Computer Programmer Group-B CBT.

PGIMER FACTS THAT CONTROL THIS RESEARCH
- Post: Computer Programmer, CP/047, Group B, PGI Satellite Centre, Sangrur
- Selection: 100 MCQs, 100 minutes, 100 marks
- English only
- Negative marking: 0.25 per wrong answer
- No interview
- PGIMER's published syllabus language says the CBT follows the prescribed
  educational qualification / professional course and experience and may include
  subjects/topics studied or practiced during that qualification/course.
- PGIMER's Computer Programmer recruitment rules specify:
  A) Master's in Statistics/Mathematics (with Statistics)/Operation Research/Physics
     OR Degree in Engineering/Computer Science or equivalent
  B) One year actual programming on an electronic computer / operation experience
  OR the alternative 7-year science-degree route.
- Desirable: formal training in programming/system design/analysis; knowledge of
  one or more programming languages.

CRITICAL CONSEQUENCE
PGIMER has NOT published a chapter-by-chapter subject list for CP/047.
Therefore no honest researcher can state that a particular topic is guaranteed.
This pack uses:
1) PGIMER's own recruitment rules and syllabus statement, and
2) actual Programmer / Computer Programmer / System Analyst recruitment papers
to build the highest-confidence subject universe.

SUBJECT 1 — C PROGRAMMING
Priority: VERY HIGH

Relevant real papers:

- Kerala PSC 2019 System Analyst/Programmer
- Kerala PSC 2024 Programmer
- Kerala PSC 2025 Programmer/System Analyst

What to prepare from these papers:

- expressions

- functions
- parameter passing
- arrays
- strings
- pointers
- pointer arithmetic
- structures/unions

- recursion
- storage classes
- scope/lifetime
- output tracing
- undefined/implementation-dependent behavior where relevant

Exam style:
Expect conceptual MCQs plus code-output / tracing questions.
Practical coding projects are NOT the priority.

============================================================
F. FINAL WARNING
============================================================

Do NOT interpret this file as saying "these 16 subjects are officially guaranteed."
The PGIMER notice does not publish that exact chapter list. These are the highest-confidence
subjects to prepare because the official PGIMER qualification/syllabus framework points to the
professional course and the actual government Programmer-family papers repeatedly test them.

For a one-vacancy exam, the correct strategy is:
official PGIMER document -> relevant past Programmer papers -> question frequency analysis ->
topic priority -> MCQ training -> timed mocks.

The next research step should be a QUESTION-LEVEL DATABASE:
extract the technical questions from these papers, tag each question by subject/topic,
count recurrence, difficulty, and question type, and use that to create the exact PGIMER
study-weight map.
